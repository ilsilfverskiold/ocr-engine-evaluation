Earlier research identified explicit absolute pixel coordinates in the exact
sent image frame, image-before-instructions ordering and recorded transforms.
The previous pixel control alone did not fix placement. The unexecuted tighter
wording was:

```text
Locate every visible word in this image. Return only a JSON array of objects:
{"text": "the printed word", "bbox": [left, top, right, bottom]}.
The supplied image is {width} pixels wide and {height} pixels high.
Coordinates are absolute pixels in this supplied image, with (0, 0) at the
top left, x increasing rightward and y increasing downward.
Give one tight rectangle around each word at its actual printed location.
Keep punctuation attached to its word. Do not combine words into line boxes.
Do not reconstruct a cleaner layout or infer positions from reading order.
Include visible headers, footers and table text. Return no Markdown or prose.
```

crop configuration on funsd_000, fintab_001 and sroie_001 before choosing one

recorded; no per-page best-answer selection. An exact call manifest and cost
