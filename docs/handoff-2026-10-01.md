## Interpretation and remaining questions

- The old KV/schema judge existed partly to handle label/format differences;
  its global substring shortcut could credit a wrong value or the right value
  under the wrong field. Current matching parses fields and rows, with
  documented normalization and limited fallbacks. Synthetic regressions do
  not quantify the full bias in the old published ranking.
- The old-to-new score delta mixes matcher changes, reference selection,
  blank policy and removal of LLM verdicts. Do not attribute that entire
  delta to substring false positives without a controlled comparison.
- All four structural policy switches remain enabled: checkbox lines as
  list items, headings in single-cell rows, list items as complete table
  cells, and table-catalogue filtering against human reference HTML. The
  table filter retains the catalogue if no table passes its overlap test.
- Blank fields count toward KV/schema accuracy. Explicit semantic-key
  precedence resolves the observed collision; repeated generic labels remain
  intrinsically ambiguous without stronger reference/output attribution.
- Image checklists and some text GT are model-authored. Reference errors,
  sampling and permissive matching can affect results. Inspect a poor score's
  saved page/output/verdict before attributing it to an engine.
- No paid work without an exact page/call/prompt/config/cost manifest and the
  owner's go. Keep scratch reports outside the repo and preserve source runs.
