Subject: Ames results on sample H81 from Hazleton Biotechnologies, Inc.

The following results were obtained for sample H81 (Firnova Powder 850.005):

# 0% S9 ACTIVATION

| strain TA9B | strain TA100 | strain 1535 | strain 1537 | strain 1538 |
| ----------- | ------------ | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| .350 (neg)  | 5.30 (neg)   | .118 (neg)  | .0887 (neg) | negative    |

# 10% S9 ACTIVATION

| strain TA98 | strain TA100 | strain 1535 | strain 1537 | strain 1538 |
| ----------- | ------------ | ----------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| .183 (neg)  | .692 (neg)   | 1.03 (neg)  | 1.05 (neg)  | .816 (neg)  |

Conclusions: The dose range tested was 0, 100, 500, 1000, 2500, 5000, and 10000 ug in 50 ul. There was no significant increase in revertant number with an increase in dose level for sample H81 with or without S9 activation for any of the 5 strains. Please feel free to discuss these results with our lab.
