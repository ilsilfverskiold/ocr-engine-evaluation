# Effectiveness of Transition Plan

# Pre-Sell

Overall pre-sell efforts were successful. Retail accounts that previously stocked Style Full Price accepted the introduction of the low price.

# BIGIF / 2 FOR 1

Proved to be an excellent tool for pulling the balance of Style Full Price packs through the system. This aided the field greatly during the transition.

# $7.00 Carton Coupon/Buydown

Effective in those retail calls that we could not exchange product out of. Those situations were limited.
