Evaluation of Borgwaldt Firmness Tester

The evaluation of the Borgwaldt Firmness Tester will determine:

- 1) If the Borgwaldt unit is as sensitive to cigarette firmness as the standard PM compacimer,
- 2) If the variability of the Borgwaldt instrument is comparable to that of the PM compacimeter,
- 3) If the Borgwaldt unit is unduly sensitive to other cigarette characteristics such as circumference or seam orientation, and
- 4) If the unit is easier to operate, set-up and maintain than the standard PM compacimeter.
