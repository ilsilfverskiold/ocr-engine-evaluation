December 6, 1954

# Dr. Hockett Joins Tobacco Group

Dr. Robert C. Hockett has been named an associate scientific director of the Tobacco Industry Research Committee. It was announced today by Dr. Clarence Cook Little, scientific director.

Dr. Hockett, a native of Fayette, Mo., will assist the committee in research in tobacco use and health. Dr. Hockett until recently was a consultant to Arthur D. Little, Inc., and to pharmaceutical, food and beverage companies. He is a former associate professor of chemistry at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

Dr. Hockett will aid Dr. Little and the Scientific Advisory Board in developing the tobacco industry's program of research into tobacco use and health, it was said. The committee recently made initial grants of $82,000 to six institutions for basic research projects and is reviewing applications for additional grants. Dr. Hockett spent nine years prior to 1952 as scientific director of the Sugar Research Foundation.

YALL STREET JOURNAL
New York, New York
December 7, 1954

# Tobacco Industry to Back Study

NEW YORK - The tobacco industry's research committee expects to spend about $60,000 in the first year on a new project it's launching to study human lung ailments.

The committee plans to give a research grant to a group of pathologists to conduct examinations of the bronchial system and the lungs. The data collected will be gathered at a central location for tabulating, comparing and analyzing. The group met here to discuss and plan the program, and will adopt specific plans at a later meeting.
